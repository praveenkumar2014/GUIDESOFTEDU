# Roadmap

0. Discovery, requirements, architecture and risk review.
1. Public site, catalogue, lead capture and admin basics.
2. Identity, applications, enrolment, learner workspace and LMS.
3. Instructor/mentor workflows, assessments and certificates.
4. Career services, employers and enterprise cohorts.

Sequence is indicative; prioritize validated user value and complete vertical slices over breadth.
