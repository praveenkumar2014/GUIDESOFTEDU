# Product Requirements — GUIDESOFT Education & AI

## Vision
Unified learning-to-career platform for professional skills, AI education, practical projects and enterprise workforce development.

## Users
Learners, instructors, mentors, employers, enterprise managers, admissions/support teams and administrators.

## Value
Learners discover programmes, learn, practise, demonstrate skills and access career support. Instructors deliver and mentor. Employers discover competencies. Enterprises train cohorts. GUIDESOFT manages admissions, academic delivery, payments and support centrally.

## Product areas
Academy, AI School, Career, Labs, Enterprise, Marketplace and Certification share one platform and identity system.

## MVP
Public site, programme catalogue, lead capture, admin lead management, authentication foundation and basic learner profile.

## Measures
Track qualified lead conversion, application completion, enrolment conversion, activation, lesson/assignment completion, assessment, satisfaction, support resolution and cohort economics. Establish baselines before setting targets.

## Launch non-goals
Microservices, Kubernetes, custom video infrastructure, guaranteed placement claims, multiple model-serving stacks and unvalidated marketplace complexity.
