# Functional Specification

## Public
Browse/search/filter programmes; inspect curriculum and delivery; submit enquiries and admissions requests.

## Identity
Least-privilege learner, instructor, mentor, employer, enterprise manager, support and admin roles. Authorization enforced server-side.

## Learner
View programmes, lessons, progress, assignments, assessments, feedback, certificates and support.

## Instructor/mentor
Manage assigned cohorts/content; review work; provide feedback; track progress.

## Employer/enterprise
Manage organization profile, opportunities, cohort participation and authorized reports.

## Admin
Manage leads, applications, programmes, users, cohorts, instructors, payments, content, support, audit logs and reports.

## Integrations
Payment, email, video, object storage and AI are adapters, configured server-side and disabled until credentials exist.

## Quality
Responsive, accessible, secure, observable, testable, privacy-conscious and resilient. Critical operations have validation, persistence and audit events.
