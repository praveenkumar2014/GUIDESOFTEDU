# Business Operating Model

Revenue options to validate: cohort programmes, self-paced programmes, enterprise learning contracts, mentoring, assessments/certification and creator marketplace commissions. Functions: marketing, admissions, academic operations, instructors, learner success, careers, enterprise sales, finance, support and compliance.

Track unit economics per programme: acquisition cost, lead-to-enrolment conversion, instructor/delivery/support cost, refund rate, completion, satisfaction and contribution margin. Set targets only after baselines exist.
