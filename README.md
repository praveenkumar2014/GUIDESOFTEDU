## Product family
GUIDESOFT Academy, AI School, Career, Labs, Enterprise, Marketplace and Certification share one platform and identity system.

## Build principles
- New GUIDESOFT product; use public market patterns only. Do not copy competitor code, protected content or assets.
- Modular monolith first; open-source-first; PostgreSQL; Next.js + TypeScript preferred.
- Preserve unrelated code/data; no destructive operations without explicit approval and verified backup.
- No secrets in Git; no fake integrations, success states, metrics or career guarantees.
- A feature is complete only when persistence, permissions, validation, errors, tests, accessibility and documentation are done.
